## What it does

{{STUDIO}} builds software that takes a large, messy body of real source material
and makes it navigable — while keeping every claim attached to the thing it came
from. And it takes on
[engineering and app development work](/services/) for other people, where the
problem is that shape.

That is not a slogan looking for a product. It is the description that fell out of
building two of them.

**[4a](/4a/)** is a daily podcast picker. Four topic queues a day rather than one
endless feed, one of them deliberately outside your usual subjects, drawn from a
library of hundreds of curated shows and tens of thousands more at breadth. It is
also building towards something harder: a **foray**, a named sequence assembled from
stretches of several episodes and played as one run.
[Three real ones are published here in full](/4a/sample/) — the clearest way to see
what the product is for.

**[longlive](/longlive/)** is a scrubbable timeline of Taylor Swift's career across
twelve eras, filterable down to music, tour, fashion or lore. Every moment is
sourced and dated, and where a narrative is a widely-held fan interpretation rather
than a confirmed fact, the site marks it on the moment itself rather than in a
disclaimer nobody reads.

Same shape, different subject. Both are about provenance under load: a lot of source
material, a reader who wants a path through it, and a hard rule that nothing gets
asserted more confidently than its source supports.

## How it works

{{ORG}} is **two founders and a fleet of AI agents. There is no other staff.**

Worth stating plainly, because it explains several things about this site that would
otherwise look like omissions: there is no team page, no office photograph, no press
list and no phone tree, and the contact address goes to a person rather than to a
queue.

It also explains what the company organises itself around. Two people cannot
out-staff anybody, so the work goes where being small and being careful beats being
large: reading a problem all the way to the bottom, and refusing to ship the version
that only looks finished.

## What we will not do

These are product principles, not aspirations. Several of them are the reason other
things are harder, and several are enforced by tests that fail the build.

**Legally boring.** 4a never rehosts, proxies, transforms or modifies podcast
audio. Playback is a seek and a stop against the publisher's own file, served from
the publisher's own host, and there is no derived audio artefact at any point. We
also do not detect, skip or strip advertising — not as a courtesy, but as a
commitment that shapes the entire design.
[The page for podcasters](/4a/for-podcasters/) is the full statement, including how
to have a show removed.

**No engagement dark patterns.** No streaks, no infinite scroll, no autoplay
chains, no notification bait. Discovery keeps a hard exploration floor, which is the
opposite of what an engagement metric would ask for — and the exploratory queue
[deliberately ignores your skip rate](/4a/#the-stretch-queue), because a slot that
backs off when ignored is a slower route to the same three shows.

**State observed, never declared.** If the software can watch something, it does
not ask. There is no settings screen where a listener has to describe themselves
before the product works.

**No third-party SDKs.** 4a contains no advertising code, no ad identifier, no
analytics library and no crash reporter. None, rather than few. Most of what it
knows [never leaves the device](/4a/your-data/), and the account it does keep holds
no name, email or phone number.

**Say less than is true, never more.** Nothing on this website describes an unbuilt
feature in the present tense, and [one page](/status/) lists what is built, what is
designed and not built, and what has not been verified. Where a decision has not
been made — a data-retention period, a compliance position — the documents record the
silence rather than filling it in, because silence with a reason is an omission and
a sentence we cannot support is a false statement.

## Where things stand

4a's web app is deployed and works, at
[jw-incorporated.github.io/foray/](https://jw-incorporated.github.io/foray/), with
no signup and no password. Native iOS and Android shells are built from the same
code; the iOS build has been launched on the simulator, the Android build has been
compiled but never run, and neither is in a store. Nothing {{ORG}} makes is
currently distributed through the App Store or Google Play. longlive is live on its
own domain.

[What is built and what is not](/status/) is the full inventory, including the
things that are designed and not built and the one thing that is written and not yet
verified.
