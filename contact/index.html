Contact

One address, for everything: help@jwlabs.dev. It is a real mailbox on this domain, and it is read by a person rather than routed into a ticket system.

What to expect

JW Labs LLC is two founders and no support staff, so the honest promise is a few days rather than a few hours. Every message is read. If a message needs a decision from both founders it can take longer, and if that happens you will get an acknowledgement rather than silence.

There is no telephone line and no live chat. We would rather say that than publish a number nobody answers.

Writing about a problem in 4a

The single most useful thing you can send is what you were doing and what happened instead of what you expected. After that:

  • Which episode, if it was one particular episode.
  • Which browser, and which device. The web app is the only released build.
  • For anything to do with audio: attach the playback diagnostics. Open the drawer in the app, choose Playback diagnostics, copy the text and paste it into the email. It records how long each load took, where playback stopped, and any control press that did not take effect. It contains no audio, no URLs and no account identifier. It exists because two playback faults were once reported from a car with no measurements attached, and there was nothing to diagnose them with.

The 4a support page answers the questions people actually ask, including how to delete your data — which is a button in the app, and emailing us will not make it happen faster or more completely.

Writing about privacy or data

Send it to the same address. 4a's privacy policy is exact about what the app stores, what it transmits and which hosts a device contacts, and §7 describes the in-app deletion control and the three things no deletion can reach. If that control reports that it could not finish, that is worth an email.

This website's own privacy notice is here. It is short, because the site sets no cookie, runs no script and has no analytics.

longlive has its own site at longlivets.com, and anything specific to it — including a correction to something on its timeline — belongs there. Company-level correspondence about it can come to the address above.
